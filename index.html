ENTER ANCIENT EGYPT
The Story

The heavens have opened. Egypt must answer.

During the reign of Queen Hatshepsut, an unnatural vortex tears across the sky. An alien mothership emerges, unleashing an invasion over ancient Egypt. With her kingdom under attack, Hatshepsut calls upon Egypt's divine protectors.
